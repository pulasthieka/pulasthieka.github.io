# Brand note — IEEE

"IEEE magazine" is treated as a genre to borrow from, not a brand to wear.

- Not used: the IEEE name as a masthead, the IEEE logo or kite mark, the IEEE Spectrum
  wordmark, or any wording that suggests the site is an IEEE publication. This is a
  personal site and must not imply affiliation.
- Used: editorial conventions that are common to engineering magazines and journals
  (lead + rail front page, department tags, two-column article page, Abstract and
  Index Terms, Fig./Table captions, numbered references, author biography).
- Colour: #00629B, a blue close to the one IEEE uses, appears as an accent in
  directions 1 and 2. It can be changed without affecting the layout.
- The one factual IEEE reference in the content is real: Endo-MASS, Best Paper Award,
  IEEE ICIIS 2023 (from the existing site).
- The masthead in every direction is the author's own name.
