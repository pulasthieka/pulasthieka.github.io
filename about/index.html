About › Where biomedical engineering and design meet

I'm Pulasthi — a biomedical engineer from Colombo, Sri Lanka. I've worked as a machine learning engineer building AI tools for scientists, and I'm now in Melbourne for a PhD, picking back up research I began during my undergrad into biosignal-based diagnostics.

Alongside the engineering, I've spent years working in UX and visual design — from web apps to posters and pitch decks. I'm especially drawn to where biomedical engineering and design meet: making complex health technology genuinely usable.
