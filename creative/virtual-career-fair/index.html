Project 01 · React · UX

Virtual Career Fair Platform

A scheduling app built in six weeks so a 40-employer university career fair could run entirely online — and be trusted by both sides of every interview.

Role
Design + front-end
Year
2021, rerun 2022
Stack
React, Firebase
Scale
40+ employers

The problem

Scheduling was running on a shared spreadsheet and a mailing list. Students didn't know which slots were real, recruiters double-booked, and organisers spent the fair on the phone reconciling both. Nobody could see the same picture at the same time.

What I did

Designed and built the whole thing in React: recruiters publish slots, students book against live availability, organisers watch the board fill. The student flow is three screens — browse, pick, confirm — and availability is the only state that ever changes on screen, so a stale slot can't be booked.

Constraints

Most students would open it once, on a phone, on university wifi, with no instructions. That ruled out onboarding, saved preferences, and anything that needed a second visit to make sense.

Outcome

Over 100 interviews scheduled in the first run with no manual reconciliation, and the faculty brought the platform back for the following year's fair.
