Research › Biosignals, diagnostics, and the models that read them

I'm a biomedical engineer (First Class Honours, University of Moratuwa) and a PhD candidate at the University of Melbourne. My research so far has centered on biosignal acquisition and diagnostics, with recent work applying deep learning to mass spectrometry and biomedical imaging.

Highlights

  • Undergraduate thesis · Univ. of Moratuwa Reconfigurable multimodal biosignal acquisition system for measuring endothelial function. Built a device recording blood pressure, temperature, bioimpedance and PPG signals, validated in a medical-council-approved clinical study. Supervised by Dr. Anjula De Silva.
  • Best Paper Award · IEEE ICIIS 2023 Endo-MASS: Multi-Signal Acquisition System and Software for Non-Invasive Assessment of Endothelial Function.
  • Industrial research · CSIRO, Brisbane Real-time 3D reconstruction for factory-floor safety. Built a reconstruction tool now used by other researchers evaluating computer vision algorithms.
  • Current work · ExpertIntelligence.ai Compound identification and quantification from mass spectrometry data. Leading research toward spatial proteomics applications.
  • Conference presentations · ASMS 2024 Oral and poster presentations. Deep learning for LC-MS authentication, and MALDI image registration.
