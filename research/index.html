Biosignals, diagnostics, and the models that read them.

Colombo → Melbourne · PhD, Oct 2026

I'm a biomedical engineer (First Class Honours, University of Moratuwa) and a PhD candidate at the University of Melbourne. My research so far has centered on biosignal acquisition and diagnostics, with recent work applying deep learning to mass spectrometry and biomedical imaging.
